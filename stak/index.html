Inside Stak

Learn by playing

Work through short lessons that show poker decisions at the table, then test your understanding.

Practice under time

Rapid Fire gives you timed questions from a separate practice bank.

Keep your progress

Track lessons, streaks, XP, and gems. Connect an account to sync progress across devices.

Your account, your choice

You can start as a guest. Connect with Google, or use Apple sign-in on iOS, when you want to save your progress. Account settings in the app let you manage sign-ins and delete your account.
